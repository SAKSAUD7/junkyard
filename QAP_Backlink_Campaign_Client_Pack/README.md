# Quality Auto Parts — Backlink Opportunity Pack

This folder is a portable working pack for the Quality Auto Parts backlink discussion. It is intentionally self-contained so it can be copied or shared without the JYNM application, backend, database, or admin portal.

## Included prospect inventory

`QAP_Prospect_List.csv` is the repository's existing stakeholder prospect sheet, copied without changing its contents. Its source sheet contains **361 rows and 358 distinct domain strings**. The source labels 292 rows “VERIFIED ACTIVE” and 69 “MANUAL REVIEW NEEDED”; those labels indicate the source sheet's domain-level check only. They do **not** demonstrate that a site accepts submissions, has approved a listing, links to QAP, or will publish an editorial backlink.

The “Estimated DA” values and suggested methods are inherited from the source CSV and have not been independently rechecked in this pack. Confirm current authority using an approved SEO data provider, inspect relevance/editorial standards, confirm that each opportunity is open to QAP, and record the live linking URL before representing any placement as completed.

## What this pack does and does not claim

- It is a research and qualification starting point, **not** a list of 361 secured backlinks.
- No backlink placements, referring-domain gains, ranking changes, or “300+ completed” placements are verified by this project folder.
- A live domain response is not evidence of a link opportunity or an actual backlink.
- No domain authority score is a Google metric or ranking guarantee.
- The 10,000-link / 45-day volume and guaranteed delivery language should not be promised as an outcome. Bulk, paid, automated, or irrelevant link placement can create search-policy and reputation risk. Use editorially earned links and legitimate business listings, and let qualified opportunities determine the pace.

## Suggested client workflow

1. Qualify each row for automotive relevance, real audience, editorial quality, geography, eligibility, and spam risk.
2. Remove duplicates and reject sites with thin/automated directories, unrelated topics, or no legitimate contribution route.
3. For each remaining prospect, document the exact opportunity and target QAP page, proposed useful contribution, owner, contact method, outreach date, response, and outcome.
4. Ask for approval before paid sponsorships, memberships, or listing fees. Mark sponsorships clearly and use appropriate link attributes where required.
5. Report separately: prospects researched, outreach sent, replies, approved placements, live linking URLs, lost links, and verified referring domains. Do not count outreach or a live website as a backlink.
6. Recheck live links and landing pages periodically; make no ranking, DA, or traffic guarantee.

## Further research needed

The prospect sheet alone does not contain current competitor backlink-gap evidence, confirmed contact/acceptance details, or published source URLs. Those must be researched and verified before it can be presented as a list of opportunities QAP can actually obtain. The source list is retained here as a transparent starting point, not silently upgraded into “verified backlinks.”
